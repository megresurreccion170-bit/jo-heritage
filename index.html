About Jo

Decades in heritage, now on your side

Jo Guard is a heritage consultant based in Victoria with a career spanning local and state government heritage practice across both the United Kingdom and Australia. After many years shaping heritage policy and decisions from within government, she founded Jo Guard Heritage in 2023 to provide independent, client-focused advice.

Jo brings a rare, inside understanding of how the heritage system actually works — from the detail of permits and consents through to strategic policy and the workings of statutory decision-making. She has served as Manager of Heritage Permits and Consents and as Heritage Operations Manager at Heritage Victoria, as a long-standing Heritage Adviser to Mitchell Shire Council, and as a member of the Heritage Council of Victoria.

That breadth means clients get advice that is both technically rigorous and genuinely pragmatic — grounded in what decision-makers look for, and focused on getting good outcomes for significant places.

Services

How Jo can help

01

Heritage advice & strategy

Clear, practical guidance for owners, developers and councils navigating heritage controls — from early feasibility conversations to considered strategy for complex or contested sites.

02

Permits & planning support

Support with heritage permit and planning permit applications, including advice on what decision-makers require and how to present a proposal for the best chance of a positive outcome.

03

Statements of heritage impact

Robust heritage impact statements that assess how proposed works affect a place's significance, prepared to the standard expected by councils and Heritage Victoria.

04

Significance assessments

Assessment of cultural heritage significance and preparation of statements of significance — the foundation for sound heritage decisions, listings and management.

05

Local government heritage

Trusted heritage adviser support for councils, drawing on years of hands-on experience advising a Victorian shire on planning referrals, policy and day-to-day heritage matters.

06

Expert & strategic review

Independent review, second opinions and strategic input on heritage listings, policy and studies — informed by experience on both sides of the statutory process.
